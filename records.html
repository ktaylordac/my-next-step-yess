📄 My Records & Transcript

Your records help your next school understand what you have already completed.

How do my records move?

YESS→Records Request→New School→Credit Review

What should I do?

1Tell us where you are goingMake sure your counselor knows your receiving school or program.
2Ask how records will be sentYour receiving school can request records through the appropriate process.
3Meet your new counselorAsk for a credit and graduation review.
4Check for missing informationIf something looks wrong, ask questions.

⭐ Advocate for Yourself

“Can we look at my credits together?”

“I think I earned this credit. Can we check?”

“What do I need to graduate?”

“Who can help me enroll?”

“What are my options?”

“Can I talk to a counselor or administrator?”

If you are unsure about a school decision, ask your counselor, parent/guardian, caseworker, or another trusted adult for help.

🏫 Going back to my boundary school

Ask for an enrollment meeting and a credit review. If you are told something you do not understand, ask for an explanation and ask your YESS counselor or another trusted adult to help you advocate for your education.
