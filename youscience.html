📄 Build My Resume

You already have experiences. Jobs, volunteer work, school projects, CTE classes, internships, sports, clubs, and skills can all help tell your story.

A simple resume can include:

  • Contact information
  • Education
  • Work experience
  • Skills
  • Certifications
  • Volunteer or community experience
  • Projects and accomplishments

Use YouScience tools, Google Docs, or an AI tool to help organize your information. Review everything before you send it.
