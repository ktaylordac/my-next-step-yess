🧠 Your YouScience Results

Your results can help you understand your strengths, interests, aptitudes, career matches, and possible education pathways.

Keep Your Results After You Leave YESS

Did you complete YouScience while you were at YESS?

Your YouScience results are YOUR information.

Even after you leave YESS, your results may continue to be useful as you:

  • 🎓 Plan for graduation
  • 🏫 Choose a college or technical school
  • 💼 Look for a job
  • 🛠️ Explore career training or apprenticeships
  • 🚀 Plan your next steps after high school
  • 💡 Learn more about what you are naturally good at

Don’t let your results get left behind!

🔐 What Happens to My YouScience Account When I Leave YESS?

Because YESS is a secure facility, you may not have been given your own school email address or YouScience password. Your counselor may have created or managed your YouScience account for you. That means you might not know the email address or password connected to your account. That’s okay!

You do NOT need to create a new YouScience account right away.

If you create a new account, your original YESS results may not be connected to it. Instead, use the steps below to try to get access to your original YouScience account and results.

🚪 How Do I Get Back to My YouScience Results?

Step 1 — Try the YouScience Login

If you know the email and password connected to your account, try logging in.

OPEN YOUSCIENCE LOGIN →

Step 2 — Don’t Know Your Login?

That’s common for YESS students. If YESS created your account and you don’t know the email or password, you may need help getting back into your original account.

Contact YouScience Support

Tell them:

“I completed YouScience through YESS in Utah. My school created my account for me, and I no longer have access to my school login. I need help accessing my original YouScience results.”

📄 Build My Resume

  • Contact information

  • Work experience
