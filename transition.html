🚦 I’m in Transition

Left YESS? Waiting for placement? Starting somewhere new? Here’s what to do next.

Step 1: Where are you right now?

🏫 I already enrolledMake sure your credits and records followed you. ⏳ I’m waitingThere are things you can do while you wait. 🤔 I don’t know yetExplore your options. 🔄 My plans changedThat’s okay. Start again.

🏫 I already enrolled in a new school

1Find your counselorAsk who your new school counselor is.
2Review your creditsMake sure the new school has the right information.
3Check your recordsAsk if your transcript and records arrived.
4Make a planAsk what classes you need next.
Check My Records

Step 2: Where might you go?

🏫 Boundary school

Ask for an enrollment meeting and a credit review. Being behind in credits does not mean you should give up on school. Ask about graduation options, credit recovery, and supports.

Try saying: “I want to stay in school. Can you help me understand my options?”

🏫 Other Granite school

Find out the exact school or program. Tell your YESS counselor where you are going and ask how your records will be transferred. Meet the new counselor and review your credits.

🏥 Treatment

Ask what education services are provided. Share your current credit information and ask how credits will be documented and transferred when you move again.

🎓 Adult Education

Do not assume you need Adult Education just because you are behind. First review your credits with your counselor and then ask an Adult Education program to review your situation.

Learn about Adult Education →
🔒 Secure care

Ask what education program you will attend, how your current credits will be shared, and how your classes will be documented.

🔄 Other alternative program

Find out the exact program, ask what records it needs, and review your credit tracker before you transition.

🛠️ Job Corps

Explore whether Job Corps fits your goals and eligibility. Compare education and career training with your other options.

❓ I’m not sure

That is okay. Start with your credits and tell your YESS counselor what you are thinking about. You do not have to decide everything today.

🔄 My plans changed

Plans change. Go back to the basics: Where are you now? What credits do you have? What records do you need? Who can help you choose the next step?
