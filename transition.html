Step 2: Where might you go?

🏫 Boundary school

Your boundary school is the school assigned to the place where you live. Ask for an enrollment meeting and a credit review. Being behind in credits does not mean you should give up on school. Ask about graduation options, credit recovery, and supports.

If your boundary school is outside Granite School District, use the Records page to learn how to send your records to them.

GO TO MY RECORDS

Try saying: “I want to stay in school. Can you help me understand my options?”

🏫 Other Granite school

If you are enrolling or re-enrolling in a boundary school in Granite School District, your YESS records will be automatically available. Meet your new counselor and review your credits together.

🏥 Treatment

Ask what education services are provided. Share your current credit tracker, and ask the program to request your transcript. Ask how credits will be documented and transferred when you move again.

🎓 Adult Education

Do not assume you need Adult Education just because you are behind. First review your credits with your counselor and then ask an Adult Education program to review your situation.

🔒 Secure care

Reach out to the Education Transition and Career Advocate (ETCA) at your secure-care location. Give them your YESS counselor’s name and YESS location so they can coordinate records. Ask who is providing your education, how your current credits will be shared, and how your classes will be documented.

🔄 Other alternative program

An alternative program may be a district alternative high school, an online or virtual school, a charter school, a specialized program, or a credit-recovery program. Find out the exact program, ask what records it needs, and review your credit tracker before you transition.

❓ I’m not sure

That is okay. If you are unsure where to go or which option is best, reach out to your YESS counselor. You do not have to decide everything today.
