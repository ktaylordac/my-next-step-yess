If you attended other schools that are not listed on your current transcript, ask your school registrar to request those records. This will help your counselor make sure all of your credits are counted toward graduation!

Know Your Graduation Requirements

The state of Utah requires students to earn a minimum of 24 credits to graduate from high school. However, some schools may require 27 credits.

For the minimum Utah state graduation requirements, view the Utah State Board of Education requirements.

Granite School District (GSD) students need 8 elective credits instead of the 5 electives listed in the state minimum requirements.

What Counts?

Not sure which classes count toward each graduation requirement?

The Utah State Board of Education (USBE) provides a guide showing the types of courses that can fulfill each of the 24 required graduation credits.

View the USBE Courses Meeting Graduation Requirements guide

Graduation Path

Behind on credits? You are not out of options.

Many students have experienced interruptions in their education, changed schools, moved between programs, or had other challenges that affected their progress. Your graduation path may look different from someone else's—and that's okay.

The important thing is knowing where you are, making a plan, and taking the next step. Ask your School Counselor for help.

How do I understand my tracker?

🟢 Green = completed

You have already earned this credit.

🟡 Yellow = enrolled or unfinished

You are enrolled in this course now, or you were enrolled before but did not finish it.

🔴 Red = still needed

You still need to earn this credit for graduation.

If a color does not make sense, ask your counselor to help you read the tracker and choose your next class.

Missing a credit?

Missing a credit does not mean you cannot graduate. Ask about credit recovery, alternative scheduling, summer options, Adult Education, or other pathways that may fit your situation.
