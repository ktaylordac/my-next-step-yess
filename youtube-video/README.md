# YouTube video package: Your Next Step Starts Here

This folder contains a ready-to-record video package for introducing the **My Next Step – YESS** website. The finished video should be about **4 minutes and 20 seconds** long.

## Files

- `script-and-storyboard.md` — narration, on-screen actions, text, and production notes
- `captions.srt` — upload-ready closed captions timed to the planned narration
- `upload-copy.md` — YouTube title, description, chapters, tags, and thumbnail direction

## Recording checklist

1. Record at 1920×1080 in a desktop browser with the browser zoom at 100%.
2. Open `https://ktaylordac.github.io/my-next-step-yess/` before recording.
3. Record the website actions in `script-and-storyboard.md` without capturing student information, browser bookmarks, notifications, or unrelated tabs.
4. Record the narration in a warm, calm, encouraging voice. Pause briefly after questions and between education paths.
5. Keep the pointer slow and visible. Use smooth cuts rather than fast zooms or flashing transitions.
6. Add the specified on-screen text. Keep it inside the center 80% of the frame so it remains readable on phones and when YouTube controls are visible.
7. Add quiet, upbeat, lyric-free music only if it does not compete with the narration.
8. Export as 1080p MP4 (H.264 video and AAC audio), then upload `captions.srt` as English captions.
9. Use the title, description, chapters, and thumbnail direction in `upload-copy.md`.
10. Watch the full export before publishing. Confirm that every page shown is current, all on-screen text is readable, captions stay in sync, and no private information appears.

## Accessibility and student-safety notes

- Do not rely on color alone to explain a choice; say and show the label.
- Read important page names aloud so students using screen readers or listening without video can follow.
- Do not use shame, fear, or promises about income. The message is that there are several valid paths and support is available.
- If contact details on the website change, rerecord that shot rather than placing an old phone number or email in the video.
- End on the website's **Get Help** page instead of displaying personal student information or asking students to comment publicly about their situation.
