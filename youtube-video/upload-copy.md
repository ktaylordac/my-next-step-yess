## Title

**Your Next Step Starts Here | How to Use the YESS Student Transition Hub**

## Description

Not sure what comes next? The My Next Step – YESS website can help you understand your credits, request school records, explore your strengths, compare education and training paths, find resources, and ask for help.

In this video, you will learn how to navigate the website and explore:

- returning to school or finding your next program
- high school credits and transcripts
- YouScience strengths and career exploration
- adult education and paths to a diploma
- technical training, two-year college, and four-year college
- financial aid and ways to pay for your future
- support for school and life outside school

You do not have to know your whole plan. You just need to know your next step.

Visit My Next Step – YESS: https://ktaylordac.github.io/my-next-step-yess/

If you need personal help, use the website's Get Help page. Please do not post private school, court, health, or contact information in the comments.
