MONEY FOR YOUR FUTURE

💰 How Will I Pay for This?

Don’t assume you can’t afford it. Find out what help may be available.

FAFSA

Federal student aid may help eligible students pay for college or career training.

Federal Student Aid →

Scholarships

Scholarships may be based on grades, interests, service, career goals, or other criteria.

Grants

Grants can help pay for education and generally do not need to be repaid when requirements are met.

Apprenticeships

Some apprenticeships let you earn money while you learn.

Employer help

Some employers help employees pay for training, certificates, or college.

Ask a financial aid office

Financial aid staff can help you understand costs and options.

Before you choose a program

  • ☐ What will it cost?
  • ☐ How long will it take?
  • ☐ What credential will I earn?
  • ☐ What jobs does it prepare me for?
  • ☐ What financial aid is available?
  • ☐ What will I need to pay myself?
