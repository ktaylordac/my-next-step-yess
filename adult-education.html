ANOTHER PATH

🎓 Behind on Credits?

You still have options. Being behind does not mean your education is over.

First: talk with someone.

Do not leave a K–12 school just because you think you are too far behind. Review your credits with your counselor and talk with an Adult Education program before making a major change.

What is Adult Education?

Utah Adult Education provides education pathways for eligible adults. Programs can review prior education and explain which completion pathway may fit your situation.

📘 Adult Education Secondary Diploma

Adult Education programs can help eligible students complete required academic credits toward an Adult Education Secondary Diploma. Ask the program to review your previous credits and explain what you still need.

📝 High School Equivalency / GED®

Utah also has a high school equivalency pathway. Eligibility and requirements depend on age and school status. Talk with an Adult Education program before deciding.

⭐ Focused Graduation Pathway

Utah has a Focused Graduation Pathway for eligible students who are significantly behind in high school credits. In an approved school program, students can use GED® exams in four subject areas to earn credits while working toward a traditional high school diploma. Ask your school counselor whether this option is available to you.

📘 Adult Education Programs

Utah Adult Education may offer Adult Secondary Education, GED® preparation, English language learning, and other support. Previous high school or adult education credits may be applied toward an Adult Secondary Diploma when allowed by program and state requirements.
