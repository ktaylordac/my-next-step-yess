⚠️ Before you apply

A real employer should not ask you to buy gift cards, send money before starting, deposit a check and return money, or share a bank password. Be careful with unexpected requests for personal information. If something feels wrong, pause and ask an adult you trust.

📜 Licenses

A license is government permission to do certain work. Training alone does not guarantee a license; you may also need an exam, supervised hours, fees, or a background check.

🚚 Commercial driver (CDL)

Why consider it: transportation jobs exist across Utah, and training can be much shorter than a degree. Complete approved entry-level training, then test through Utah Driver License Division.

Utah CDL steps →

✂️ Cosmetology or barbering

Why consider it: hands-on work with options in salons, shops, or self-employment. Complete required school or apprenticeship hours, pass exams, and apply through DOPL.

License information →

⚡ Electrician or plumber

Why consider it: earn wages during an apprenticeship and build a portable skilled trade. Register, complete supervised work and classes, then meet DOPL exam requirements.

Electrician steps →
Plumber steps →

💊 Pharmacy technician

Why consider it: enter health care with focused training rather than a four-year degree. Complete Utah’s education or training requirements and apply through DOPL.

Pharmacy licensing →

🚑 EMT

Why consider it: gain emergency-care experience that can lead toward fire, paramedic, or health careers. Take an approved course, pass required testing, and apply for Utah certification.

🏠 Real estate sales agent

Why consider it: flexible, people-focused work with commission-based earning potential. Complete approved pre-license education, pass the exam, and apply through DRE.

Check the official agency before enrolling. Requirements and fees can change. A school should clearly explain whether its program qualifies you for Utah licensure.

🏅 Certifications

A certification shows that you learned a skill or passed an industry test. It is different from a government license. Ask YESS staff which options are available in secure care; availability can depend on your site and schedule.

Food Handler Permit

Useful for restaurants, cafeterias, and food service. Complete training from a Utah-approved provider and pass its assessment.

Approved Utah providers →

CPR, AED & First Aid

Useful in child care, recreation, health, and public-facing jobs. Take an instructor-led or blended course and complete the skills check.

Find Red Cross training →

OSHA 10-Hour Card

Introduces workplace safety for construction or general industry. Take a class from an OSHA-authorized provider. It does not replace employer-required training.

Find authorized training →

Certified Nursing Assistant

Useful for entry-level patient care and a first step toward nursing. Complete a Utah-approved program, then pass the state skills and knowledge exams.

Utah Nursing Assistant Registry →

🔧 Technical School

Utah technical colleges teach hands-on skills for specific jobs. Programs can take weeks, months, or longer and may lead to a certificate, license, or apprenticeship.

Utah’s public technical colleges

Bridgerland, Davis, Dixie, Mountainland, Ogden-Weber, Southwest, Tooele, and Uintah Basin Technical College offer programs based on regional employers’ needs.

Compare Utah technical colleges →

Ask about admission age, high-school enrollment, program prerequisites, tuition, tools, financial aid, and job placement before choosing.

🎓 2-Year College

A two-year college offers associate degrees and shorter certificates. You can prepare for a job or transfer credits toward a bachelor’s degree.

Salt Lake Community College

Admission: open admission for most programs; apply, send transcripts, complete placement steps if needed, and meet any program-specific requirements.

Apply to SLCC →

Snow College

Admission: submit the undergraduate application and transcript. Some programs may have extra requirements.

Apply to Snow →

These are Utah’s public colleges primarily offering two-year degrees. Utah’s universities also offer some associate degrees. Confirm deadlines and program requirements with the school.

🪖 Military

Military service is a serious commitment. Learn about the job, service obligation, pay, benefits, physical standards, and risks before deciding. A recruiter can explain options, but you do not have to sign anything at your first meeting.

Active-duty branches

  • Army
  • Marine Corps
  • Navy
  • Air Force
  • Space Force
  • Coast Guard

The Army and Air National Guard serve both Utah and the nation. Most branches also have Reserve components.

Compare branches →

How to get started

  1. Compare branches and jobs.
  2. Talk with a trusted adult and more than one recruiter.
  3. Ask about age, diploma/GED, citizenship, medical, legal, and ASVAB requirements.
  4. Get promises about your job or benefits in writing before enlisting.

Questions to ask about any path

What am I good at?

Use YouScience and your experiences.

What will it really cost?

Compare tuition, fees, tools, transportation, housing, and available aid.

What result will I get?

Ask how long it takes, what credential you earn, and whether employers or Utah licensing agencies accept it.
