YOUR FUTURE

🌎 What Could I Do After High School?

There is more than one good path. Find a path that fits your goals, strengths, needs, and life.

💼WorkStart building experience and income.
🛠️ApprenticeshipEarn while you learn a skilled trade.
📜LicenseMeet requirements for a licensed career.
🏅CertificationShow skills for a specific industry.
🔧Technical SchoolHands-on training for a career.
🎓2-Year CollegeCertificates, associate degrees, and transfer options.
🎓4-Year CollegeBachelor’s degrees and many career options.
🪖MilitaryExplore service, training, benefits, and requirements.

College is one option — not the only option.

Start with what interests you. Then research the education or training that the career requires.

Questions to ask

What am I good at?

Use YouScience and your experiences.

What does it cost?

Compare tuition, tools, transportation, and living costs.

How long will it take?

Look at certificates, licenses, degrees, and apprenticeships.
